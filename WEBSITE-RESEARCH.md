# Agrotech Agriculture — Website research and design brief

Research date: 21 September 2026.
Reference: https://alqasimagroeng.com/
Scope: reference-site research and a build-ready brief; no website implementation or publication yet.

## 1. Project direction

Agrotech Agriculture manufactures agricultural machinery, including laser land levelers, according to the project owner. Its complete product range, location, history, specifications and service commitments still require company information.

Build a straightforward manufacturer website that helps a farmer identify a machine, understand whether it suits their tractor and work, and contact the company. Prioritize product enquiries, phone calls and WhatsApp. An online checkout is not part of the proposed first release.

The requested visual character is simple, clear and predominantly white. Achieve a professionally designed, natural appearance through real photography, restrained typography, useful information and consistent spacing.

### Improvements to carry into our design

- Header “Get a quote” and the inspected leveler page's “Learn More” link point to the homepage. Agrotech actions should lead directly to an enquiry route with the selected product retained. Source: [leveler page](https://alqasimagroeng.com/product/laser-land-leveler/).
- Several footer product links also point to the homepage. Each Agrotech footer link should open its named product. Source: [blog page/footer](https://alqasimagroeng.com/blog/).
- The reference leveler gallery occupies most of the first desktop screen. Our detail page should place the image beside a short product summary and enquiry action.
- Specification coverage varies between products. Use one consistent product data template, with only applicable fields.
- Reference website and PDF specifications are not always aligned: for example, Super Seeder tractor power is shown as 65 HP on the page and 75/65 in the PDF. Use a single approved Agrotech specification record for both web and downloadable documents. Sources: [Super Seeder](https://alqasimagroeng.com/product/super-seeder/) and [PDF](https://alqasimagroeng.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Al-Qasim-Presentation.pdf).
- Correct catalogue spelling, use specific product descriptions, and omit unfinished articles.

Review limits: desktop homepage and leveler page were visually inspected; main pages and all nine product detail pages were read. Form delivery, video playback, every download, mobile layouts and measured performance were not tested. No enquiries were submitted. Reference company claims are not independently verified.

## 4. Homepage sequence

1. **Header:** logo on the left, conventional navigation, phone or quote action on the right.
2. **Hero:** one strong photograph of an Agrotech machine working, a brief factual headline and two actions: “View machinery” and “Request a quote”. Use live text rather than lettering baked into a banner.
3. **Featured machinery:** Laser Land Leveler first, followed by confirmed products. Each entry has a clean image, name, one-line use and detail link.
4. **Manufacturing introduction:** a short paragraph beside an authentic workshop photograph. Describe actual capabilities.
5. **Leveler demonstration:** a still image with a play button and a short explanation; load the video when requested.
6. **Ownership and support:** confirmed spare-parts, setup, delivery or service arrangements in plain language.
7. **Catalogue strip:** a compact download link when the brochure is available.
8. **Contact and footer:** direct sales contact, address, opening hours, useful links and verified social accounts.

Sample headline direction: “Laser land levelers and agricultural machinery.” Supporting copy should describe the actual range and service area once supplied. Avoid unsupported promises about savings, yield, awards or market leadership.

## 5. Visual specification

These are proposed design choices, not colours sampled from Agrotech branding.

| Element | Proposed direction |
| --- | --- |
| Main canvas | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Secondary surface | Very light grey `#F5F6F4`, used sparingly |
| Main text | Charcoal `#252A27` |
| Secondary text | Grey `#5B635E` |
| Primary accent | Deep agricultural green `#245D3A`, provisional pending logo |
| Borders | Soft grey `#E1E5E0` |
| English typography | Arial / Helvetica / sans-serif; regular body, bold headings |
| Urdu typography | A readable Urdu-capable font, checked with actual text and right-to-left layouts |
| Body copy | Approximately 16–18 px, comfortable line spacing |
| Desktop headings | Approximately 32–44 px for the main heading and 26–32 px for sections |
| Buttons | Solid accent, clear labels, modest 4–6 px rounding |
| Layout | Approximately 1160–1200 px maximum content width; orderly columns and consistent spacing |
| Photographs | Real equipment, operators and factory; consistent crops and natural colours |

If the company logo and equipment favour red, use one restrained red accent instead of green. Avoid combining multiple competing brand colours.

The requested human-made feel means no decorative glowing shapes, glass panels, oversized slogan walls, excessive pill badges, continuous animations or repetitive generic feature cards. Use ordinary headings such as “Our machinery”, “Technical specifications” and “Contact our team”. No single font determines quality; the result depends on proportion, writing, imagery and detail.

## 6. Product information template

Every product should provide:

- Product name, model code and short description of the work it performs.
- Three to six genuine photographs: full machine, alternate angle, important components and field use.
- Available models and sizes, with consistent units.
- Required tractor power and attachment compatibility where applicable.
- Dimensions, weight and working width; PTO/hydraulic details where relevant.
- Included equipment versus optional accessories.
- Short benefits grounded in verified machine capabilities.
- Demonstration video and brochure if available.
- Confirmed warranty, parts and service information.
- Price or a clear quotation route, according to company policy.
- “Ask about this machine” action that carries the product and model into the enquiry.

For the **Laser Land Leveler**, specifically request blade/bucket width and capacity, tractor requirements, hydraulic requirements, transmitter/receiver/controller details, mast adjustment, stated accuracy and conditions, included tripod/accessories, installation/calibration support and available model differences. Publish only manufacturer-approved values; these are data requests, not claims about Agrotech equipment.

## 7. Enquiry and mobile experience

Quote form: name, mobile number, city/district, product/model and optional message. Email can be optional. Keep tractor model/HP as an optional field if it helps sales advise the customer.

Product pages should preselect the relevant machine. WhatsApp messages can include the product name and page URL. Configure the receiving phone and email only from confirmed Agrotech details.

The form must actually deliver enquiries, show clear success/failure feedback, validate required fields and control spam. Provide a direct phone alternative. Explain how submitted contact details will be used.

On phones: collapse navigation into a simple menu, use one or two product columns according to available width, preserve readable machine images, and provide reachable Call/WhatsApp actions without covering content. Make specification tables readable with labelled horizontal scrolling or stacked model details. Avoid autoplay video and large rotating banners.

Language is pending. Recommended approach: plan for English and Urdu, with proper Urdu navigation and right-to-left layouts if bilingual launch is chosen. Do not publish incomplete machine translations.

## 8. Build and launch requirements

Use reusable header, footer, product listing and product detail templates. Keep product specifications in one structured source so updates remain consistent.

Platform choice should follow the editing requirement: a managed CMS is useful if staff will update models regularly; a lightweight custom site can suit a relatively stable catalogue. No framework, hosting subscription or domain choice is committed by this research.

Required implementation qualities:

- Responsive, accessible navigation and visible keyboard focus.
- Readable text contrast, labelled controls and descriptive image alternatives.
- Properly sized/compressed images; defer below-page media and video embeds.
- Individual product URLs, useful page titles/descriptions and crawlable content.
- Sitemap, canonical URLs and accurate business details; include structured data only for true, available information.
- Secure enquiry handling with server-side validation and no exposed credentials.
- A documented way to edit product data, photos and contact information.
- Before launch, test navigation, quote delivery, phone/WhatsApp links, downloads, mobile widths, keyboard access, missing pages and page loading.
